What it catches

  • Exact copies — matched by SHA-256, bit for bit
  • Resized copies — in our seeded test, a half-size copy measured perceptual distance 0 out of 64
  • Re-compressed copies — a re-saved JPEG measured distance 2; unrelated photos measured 38
  • Adjustable sensitivity — threshold 0–32, default 6. Stricter or wider net, your call, and the report shows the distance behind every match
